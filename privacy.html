Data protection

Privacy notice

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this consultation is UK Young Ambassadors. You can contact us at —.

What we collect

The form asks for:

  • your age band, and the nation or region of the UK you live in — or the country, if you live elsewhere;
  • optionally, the first part of your postcode, so we can see which areas we have reached;
  • your answers about policy, including free-text answers written in your own words;
  • optionally, equalities information: gender, ethnic background, disability or long-term health condition, care experience, and what you are currently doing.

We do not ask for your name, your email address, or your full postcode, and the form does not record which Google account you are signed in to. Every question except the ones marked required can be skipped, and every equalities question has a “prefer not to say” option.

Our lawful basis

We process your answers on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the box at the end of the form. Equalities information is special category data and is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, for the purpose of equality of opportunity monitoring. You can withdraw consent at any time — see “your rights” below.

What we do with it

  • We publish totals, averages and charts on this website. Individual responses are never published.
  • We write a report for UK government departments and for Commonwealth youth structures. It contains the same aggregated figures.
  • We may quote free-text answers in the report and on this website. Quotations are anonymous and we remove anything that could identify you or anyone you mention. If an answer cannot be anonymised, we do not use it.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share individual responses with any third party.

Where it is stored

Responses are collected using Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets. Google acts as our processor and may store data outside the UK; those transfers rely on the safeguards in Google’s own data processing terms. Access to the response spreadsheet is limited to named members of the delegation and is protected by two-factor authentication.

How long we keep it

Individual responses are deleted 24 months after the consultation closes. The aggregated results and the published report are kept indefinitely, because they are a public record of what young people said — but they contain no individual responses.

If you are under 13

This consultation is for people aged 13 to 25. Please do not fill in the form if you are under 13. The age question includes an “under 13” option only so that we can identify and delete such responses before analysis, which we do.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. There is an honest limitation here: because responses are anonymous, we usually cannot find your individual response in order to delete it. If you want a response removed, contact us as soon as possible with the approximate date and time you submitted it and as much detail as you can remember, and we will do our best to identify and delete it.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or by calling 0303 123 1113.
