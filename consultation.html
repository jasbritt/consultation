The questions in full

Published up front so you can see exactly what is being asked and how it will be analysed before you start. Nothing here is a trick question and there are no right answers.

Why we ask you to rate the same areas three times

Asking only “how important is this?” produces a list on which everything is important. So each of the twelve policy areas is rated on three different things: how well it works today, how much it matters to your long-term future, and how urgent it is right now.

The useful number is the distance between them. An area rated critically important and barely working is a delivery failure; one rated urgent but not important long term is a crisis to manage; one rated important long term but not urgent is where prevention money should go. That analysis is what makes the report usable by a department rather than just interesting.

Your privacy

The form does not collect your name or email address, and does not record your Google account. Demographic questions are optional and every one has a “prefer not to say” option.

Answers are reported only in aggregate. Free-text answers may be quoted in the published report, with any identifying detail removed. Data is held by UK Young Ambassadors for 24 months and then deleted.

Running a session with young people?

The questions work well as a 45-minute workshop: use the “bring it back” and “biggest problem” questions as discussion prompts, then have everyone complete the ratings individually so the numbers stay independent of the loudest voice in the room.
